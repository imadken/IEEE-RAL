## Full ALFWorld monitoring-interface limitation

The native task model contains disjunctive/existential preconditions, conditional
effects, action costs, and observation actions with checked-state effects.
The feedback grammar depends on state and ordered rule fallbacks. A parser that
maps every phrase to an intuitive Boolean can be unsound; incomplete descriptions
must not imply closed-world negatives.

Concrete boundary case from actual source and execution: ToggleObject toggles
isOn conditionally and sets isToggled unconditionally, while toggleObject.feedback
and examineObject's "is on" phrase test isToggled. Two native `use desklamp1`
commands both returned "You turn on..."; audited facts contained isToggled and
not_isOn. This is evidence against treating the literal phrase as isOn=true;
do not generalize it into an unverified paper-wide bug claim or alter benchmark
semantics. isToggled and isOn are distinct declared predicates.

Evidence: /tmp/monitoring_alfworld_observation_audit.py and
/tmp/monitoring-alfworld-observation-audit.json. This is an interface audit, not
an uncertainty regime chosen to favor any monitoring method.

Conservative parsing is possible for narrow inventory, open/closed and positive
containment cases after inspecting corresponding grammar. A complete checked
feedback inversion, timestamp handling, belief update and ADL-to-grounded model
adapter was NOT validated within this compatibility worker. Recommendation:
keep actual3/3 native ALFWorld smoke distinct from the comparative qualification;
report full ALFWorld monitor integration incomplete. Do not label exposed-state
PDDL or ideal-symbolic-sensor experiments as native ALFWorld sensing comparisons.
