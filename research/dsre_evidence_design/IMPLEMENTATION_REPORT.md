## Implemented method and scope

`src/dual_rec/evidence_aware/` contains immutable scope/record contracts, four-cell bounds, discovery partitions, certificates, constructive diagnoses, source-separated views, baselines, acquisition and CLI commands. Production linear optimization enumerates active-set vertices after eliminating q00. It does not import the supervisor's HiGHS solver. Exact binomial intervals use the prescribed per-tail `.025/(4K)` allocation for each of the two evidence blocks. Unknown causal assumptions, absent overlap, inconsistent sets and mismatched outcome scopes fail closed.

The primary request remains `harm <= .10` and `gain >= .05`; completion-only, harm-only and tolls `0/.02/.05` remain distinct secondary requests. Deployment defaults to the identical continue macro whenever recovery is unqualified. Deferral is reported separately without assigning physical failure to unexecuted actions. No toll, outcome definition, seed or risk threshold was selected from confirmation outcomes.

The partition enumerates depth-at-most-two Boolean trees, with at most four leaves, minimizing observed-arm Bernoulli squared error and requiring eight discovery observations per arm in each split leaf. Every method uses the same partition fitted from single-action discovery labels. Certificates exhaustively minimize literal count over conjunctions constant on every complete Boolean leaf, requiring all selected leaves to pass. Scope includes the task, controller, horizon, observation protocol and selected whole leaves. Each retained literal has a logical removal obstruction. Minimum cardinality is restricted to this declared grammar, not all explanations or minimum acquisition cost.

Scoped soundness is the specification's elementary argument: conditional on independent discovery, simultaneous CP containment includes each true regional law in its ambiguity set with probability at least .95 per task; extrema then imply the requested regional inequalities. A leaf-constant router and a conjunction selecting whole passing leaves inherit those inequalities by mixtures. Finer routing or changed missingness/sensing protocols do not inherit them automatically. Adding valid constraints on the same target set refines bounds; changing populations or sensors is not automatically refinement. These are known confidence/optimization arguments and remain subject to supervisor assumption review, not a new-theorem claim.
