## I. Value of hypothesis and differentiation

The worthwhile question is whether diagnosis changes a *fixed executable
feedback-recovery catalogue* beyond elapsed time, and whether accounting for
that change improves completion. The equal-duration hold and common remaining
deadline give this question an operational intervention contrast. Information
gain, state-constraint compliance and recovery feasibility are different objects.
Losing one macro is not losing all available macros; neither implies loss of all
possible robot controllers. Report creation as well as loss of options.

The strongest novelty objection is substantive: contingent/reach-avoid planning
can represent the objective, and established task-aware sensing already connects
identification with downstream action. The defensible contribution is the
postfailure, time-controlled catalogue-relative measurement and its population
consequences. A running planner or a finite counterexample does not demonstrate
a new solver, empirical prevalence or superiority to the same-objective planner.
DA-GRD explicitly permits contact-induced object motion. Kobayashi explicitly
connects identification with finite-horizon control. These capabilities must
remain acknowledged, including equivalent task-consensus adaptations.

H1 must be risky in both directions. With selectors frozen before confirmation,
a small upper bound on selected-policy q_IG limits that selector. It says little
about an unselected probe; a small selected-probe rate can arise from abstention.
Report rho_IG, rho_H, both fixed-probe q_p and conditional q_IG/rho_H when defined.
Only simultaneous fixed-probe bounds justify a limited statement about those
probes in that population. Even those do not establish universal physical
benignity. Zero erasures make eta_gamma undefined; they do not prove that probe
responses lack information.

A precise negative H1 result can rule out the proposed material prevalence in
the declared population. A negative H2 result can exclude the prespecified gain
only if its confidence support is sufficiently narrow. Inconclusive intervals,
unqualified source or an empty hold catalogue cannot be relabeled falsification.
The historical failed source provides none of these confirmatory conclusions.
