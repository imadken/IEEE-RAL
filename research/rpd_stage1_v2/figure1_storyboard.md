# Figure 1 storyboard

Scientific thesis: a physical diagnostic action can distinguish more hypotheses yet
remove corrective policies that would have remained executable after waiting.

One explicitly constructed, clock-augmented three-world model. Uniform prior over
A, B, C; two recovery policies a0, a1. All three branches take 0.5 seconds from a
checkpoint with 4 seconds remaining. All illustrated paths satisfy the nominal
allowed-state constraint. The shown option sets are evaluated with 3.5 seconds
remaining. They are not simulated robot measurements or new physical findings.

Reading order:

1. Ambiguous checkpoint, common initial support.
2. Three offline matched branches: physical hold, fine probe, coarse probe.
3. Response partition, information in nats, and the complete declared option sets.
4. Fine probe loses just a0 in B, but removes C's only option, a1, leaving an
   empty catalogue. Red EMPTY distinguishes
   full erasure from partial loss. This does not prove arbitrary physical
   irreversibility.
5. Online execution follows only one branch. Coarse response AB selects a0; C
   selects a1. The evaluator's three branches are never online observations.

The fine response gives ln(3)=1.0986122887 nats, coarse gives
H(2/3,1/3)=0.6365141683 nats, hold gives zero. These are analytical mutual
information values under the declared finite model, distinct from the proposed
physical study's held-out log-score contrast.
