# DSRE: bounded primary-method challenge

The question remains minimum observable evidence for adequately good fixed recovery
interventions. This ledger is a targeted comparison, not an exhaustive novelty
clearance or a manuscript. Primary method sections were read before scoring DSRE
methods. No model/checkpoint, external dataset, author contact or issue posting was
used. The missing blueprint means its exact URL/version list cannot be checked;
the sources below were located by their fixed titles/authors from the execution
contract. Bibliographic acceptance is stated only where primary proceedings identify
it; the three 2026 arXiv works remain preprints here.

## Exact equivalences and novelty boundary

For binary V and v=1, Accept_i contains exactly the successful measured actions.
Consequently DSRE is a minimum-cardinality pure outcome-conditioned conjunction with
conservative UNKNOWN matching and minimum support. **B7 is the same decision problem**;
its missing witness renderer is an interface difference. B5 with conditional mean
success threshold1 is also identical when supports, candidate grammar and tie-breaks
match. Exhaustive enumeration/hitting sets and deletion necessity witnesses do not
constitute new general theory. A stochastic or average-outcome variant would change
the question; none is introduced to escape these equivalences.

The potentially useful synthesis is qualified intervention data + explicit adequate-
action sets + exact small certificates/insufficiency + independently calibrated
router. Software correctness cannot establish a publication-worthy scientific gap.
The final gate must consider the actual simple-baseline frontier and identifiability,
even if they make the proposed method indistinguishable from a stump or pure rule.
The older CASE/RA-L-style evidence-contract work remains provenance infrastructure,
not a novelty exemption; no claim of comprehensive CASE/RA-L coverage or clearance
is made. The fixed nine closest-method readings and two robotics context readings
are the bounded scope, with no additional topic search.
