## Evidence semantics and physical interpretation

Exact minimum-literal certificates establish a common adequate action over
compatible frozen reference rows, conditional on trustworthy observed facts
and source support. Typed observation insufficiency identifies conflicting
adequate actions under the available observations; repertoire insufficiency
identifies the absence of an adequate available skill; reference insufficiency
prevents missing support from becoming a positive certificate. These meanings
must remain separate from a test-outcome hindsight ceiling and from a claim of
population safety. B7's exact rule equivalence is part of the method statement.

The earlier observation adapter reduced false asserted literals within its
declared 0/1/5-mm error set. It did not eliminate inadequate proposals: the
5-mm condition still had five. Robust factual support and independently
calibrated action eligibility are different claims. The present clean source
gate does not transfer to noise, arbitrary checkpoints or real robots.

The new registered unit is an independent parent: an action stratum is assigned
when any of its five correlated conditions receives that proposal, and errors
are the union of failed assigned continuations within that parent. This
conservative action-specific definition, complete-cohort requirement and exact
simultaneous bounds were not relaxed. With no assigned parents the upper=1
convention expresses no information; it is not an estimated failure probability.

## Independent scientific critique and remaining weaknesses

The integrity audit passes and the historical numerical reproduction passes.
The physical qualification fails: a direct unchanged prefix probe segfaults in
SAPIEN material construction without using the policy scorer. The underlying
cause is unknown. Package/source identity and passing numerical tests cannot
establish controller-memory, contact, sensor or terminal-outcome parity in an
environment that cannot construct its first scene. This is the decisive gate.

The strongest anticipated empirical contribution—source-specific independently
calibrated selective recovery with held-out necessary-recovery recall—is absent.
Neither n=0 risk bounds nor successful verification of older labels fills that
gap. The older DSRE risk versus CART2 proposal tradeoff (more adequate actions
and necessary recoveries, but also more inadequate actions) supports no new
equal-risk superiority claim. Mathematical equivalence to ordinary rules limits
algorithmic novelty and must remain explicit.

Even a later completed study would be conditional on independent simulator
parent resets and the fixed five-condition distribution. It would establish
neither real-world safety nor an individual-checkpoint guarantee. The conservative
assignment support may refuse recovery qualification; that would be a valid
scientific result. The present technical failure is not that result.

The observed old feasibility/justification gap remains a useful, precisely
scoped finding. Certificates and witnesses explain the limits of a fixed
observation/action/reference interface. They do not add recovery ability beyond
the same fixed skills, nor demonstrate a controller advantage. Any future
physical difference must be decomposed into required recovery, maintenance,
unnecessary harm and any additional executed prefix before claiming improvement.
