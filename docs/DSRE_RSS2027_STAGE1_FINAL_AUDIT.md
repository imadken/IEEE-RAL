## What changed scientifically

The research question and mathematical method are fixed. The actual paper now separates (1) measured physical potential from paired actions, (2) preregistered selective credit with STOP=0, and (3) finite evidential justification and separately qualified statistical eligibility. Its opening diagram grounds the four outputs in actual cases; the main figure puts raw ungated branches and gated credit on distinct sides of a divider. Page 5 now carries ceilings, witnesses, robustness and limitations rather than mostly blank space. No numerical metric was substituted for the primary endpoint.

The additional STOP/fallback analysis is explicitly post hoc secondary. All original primary estimates and parent quantiles are preserved. Successful-at-defer observations do not become hold outcomes. Both predeclared-for-reanalysis fallback directions are shown with descriptive errors and no inherited qualification. Simple fallback removes Pick's gap with continue and Push's with recover; ordinary rules and trees remain tied. This narrows rather than strengthens any claim of controller superiority.
