## Independent reviewer criteria

1. **Source validity:** five supported insertion predicates only; aperture UNKNOWN; fresh pilot and explicit first differences; actual source endpoint or MISSING. No invented sensor, horizon repair, seed replacement or selection of favorable complete cases.
2. **Prospective validity:** original disjoint cohorts, genuine randomized discovery/calibration, partition/proposals fixed before calibration, calibration/acquisition schedules independent of their outcomes, all models frozen before test. Evaluation counterparts never enter the logged learner.
3. **Statistical validity:** fixed primary harm<=.10 and gain>=.05; original simultaneous tail allocation; actual whole-leaf support; one independent condition per parent; missingness and unexecuted parents distinct. Descriptive curves and bootstrap intervals do not become simultaneous significance claims.
4. **Scientific value:** at least one genuine useful qualified recovery on fresh non-grasp test; source/scope-correct comparisons; meaningful evidence-repair effect or a sharply delimited non-identification result. Passing software tests alone satisfies none of these empirical requirements.
5. **Truthful comparison:** ordinary rules/direct bounds may match exactly. No action-superiority claim for equivalent methods. Matched CART and proposal sensitivities remain visible; literature adaptations retain their labels.

G6 remains a supervisor decision. If these conditions are not met, use the contract's scientific disposition (`VALID_BUT_UNDISTINGUISHED` or `INCOMPLETE_SOURCE_EVIDENCE`, as applicable), retain `SCIENTIFIC_ADVANCE_NOT_ESTABLISHED` as the original ledger gate, and leave both manuscripts untouched.
