## Stage 1 thesis and evidence requirements

Ask when a fixed recovery intervention can be justified from measured current facts and a declared offline outcome-evidence source. The scientific object is the region-scoped obligation-specific certificate, together with a constructive reason for withholding one. Report independent action capacity, observational ambiguity, causal/source identifiability and finite calibration support; do not present an additive decomposition.

Use only claims surviving the final report's H1/H2/H3 assessment. A numerical same-marginal witness establishes a methodological distinction; it does not establish frequent paired-only robot regions. If actual physical regions are already log-certifiable or statistical support is weak, state that. If ordinary rules and CART implement identical actions, present their equality in one fair matched comparison. Do not advertise a new bound, solver, universally safer controller or evidence-acquisition advantage.

The proposed scientific presentation has three connected claims: (1) source and obligation change what can be warranted, including nonidentification; (2) minimum facts over whole independently calibrated cells preserve the declared finite-sample scope; (3) source-qualified robot executions expose both useful interventions and unnecessary/harmful or futile interventions. Distinguish evidence for each claim from its assumptions, simulator-only reference and failed optional qualification.

The thesis figure should show current facts, offline evidence type, compatible outcome distributions and the named obligation, then either a minimum certificate or an insufficiency witness and one executed action. Its inset can use the two analytical q tables with identical marginals and different harm. Its lower empirical panel must show actual task-specific denominators. A paired branch is labeled offline evaluation only. Scientific figures delivered by this campaign are data/obligation figures, not a software-component inventory.

## Feasible later evaluation, subject to separate review

The extension question must remain intervention-evidence sufficiency. A later reviewed protocol could qualify a further independent contact family, with uniform no-checkpoint handling and a preregistered substitution rule if the source cannot support insertion. It must use fresh calibration and untouched tests rather than relabeling this pilot. Any alternative mechanism requires the later reviewers' authorization and is not an experiment in this session.

A second feasible extension is a larger randomized one-arm study with controlled observation missingness, fixed measurement protocols and matched action cost. A third, optional extension may add an already qualified fixed macro to a finite recovery repertoire and compare obligations against the same continue baseline. Neither is a Stage 1 dependency or a promise of hardware access. An expanded evidence-identification/cost frontier should retain strong no-harm and exact-rule comparisons, including identical decisions as a valid result.

No current failure authorizes changing controllers, searching another fault severity, adding a new task, retuning after held-out results, or restarting the old blocked insertion1024/256 study.
