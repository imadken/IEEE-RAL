## 7. Exact pure rules and counterexamples are classical. What is the new insight?

Valid novelty limit, explicitly acknowledged in Secs. II–III. The intervention outcome target differs from preserving a predictor label. The falsifiable robotics findings are that stronger observation mostly maintains successful starts, finite purity does not establish risk eligibility, selected skill quality can be high while strict coverage is zero, and a stable action can carry factually contradicted evidence. Ordinary-rule equality is retained. Whether that scoped systems characterization is sufficiently significant for RSS remains a human scientific judgment.
