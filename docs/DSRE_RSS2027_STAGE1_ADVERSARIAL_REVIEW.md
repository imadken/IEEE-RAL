## A. What is distinct from B7 and partial-observability feasibility?

This is the strongest unresolved objection. Strict DSRE and B7 enumerate the same conjunctions, support and ties; the risk wrappers also coincide mathematically. The invariant adapter does not change this identity. Zhu and Leonard already characterize feasibility through action-set intersection over observation-consistent states. The elementary refinement property is an instance of that principle. Minimal explanations, outcome-aware rules, interval reasoning and paired recovery evaluation are established separately.

The revised Sections II–III state this directly. The narrower proposed contribution is the operational robotic evidence contract: minimum observed proof objects tied to measured alternatives, three counterexample types, declared measurement invariance, and a separately scoped action-risk interface. The study tests whether those obligations actually disagree. It is not a new search algorithm or feasibility theorem. Whether this integration and mechanism evidence are sufficiently distinct for RSS remains a principal-researcher judgment. Numerical compliance cannot resolve that objection.

## B. Is the five-arm variation meaningfully physical?

Yes within a narrow controlled family. The offsets change an existing scripted approach target, with replay/contact/controller/condition and repeated-branch parity qualification. They do not translate the object or change materials, controllers or task definitions. Every registered arm and all 192 primary parents completed; the 128-parent confirmation cohort is separate from characterization and historical data. Nominal recovery harms 27 states and has no recover-only state; +3 cm recovery rescues 108 failed starts while both skills fail on 20. These are different intervention requirements rather than sensor-only relabeling.

Ecological breadth remains limited: one new simulator task, idealized telemetry, two scripted skills, and five settings of one approach parameter. Earlier Push evidence is reused exploratorily, not presented as a second new prospective task. Figure 1's Push witnesses are explicitly historical. The paper cannot establish hardware or general fault robustness.

## C. Is this required recovery or mainly maintenance?

Both must remain visible. Zero-radius risk DSRE selects 48/238 failed-start required recoveries, with 50 total proposed recoveries: 48 necessary, one both-success maintenance, one neither-success failure. It selects no harmful recovery on a continue-only state. The other inadequate action is continuation on a preventive recover-only state. CART selects 35 required recoveries, with one fewer inadequate action overall. The paired adequate-credit difference is +2.03 pp, CI [0.94,3.28], not certified dominance.

The risk-versus-strict increment of 50 adequate actions is entirely maintenance; it adds no required recovery. The text now states this rather than using additional selective credit as a recovery-improvement headline. At 5 mm, risk DSRE selects only 15/238 required recoveries and makes five inadequate continuations. Fixed recovery attains 523 raw successes, above DSRE's 401, but incurs 117 inadequate interventions including 69 harmful recoveries. STOP receives zero selective credit, with no invented hold/human-escalation outcomes.

A further independent finding limits the new-grid aliasing interpretation: its seven-fact continue-only/recover-only conflict involves the single preventive case. No continue-only/failed-start recover-only pair shares all seven facts in this cohort. The paper discloses this; it does not attribute the full 336-state observation gap to indistinguishable necessary-recovery conflicts. Many impure classes include neither-success states. The previously qualified Push pair supplies a genuine initially unsuccessful opposing-action witness.

## D. Is measurement invariance being sold as action safety?

No. Section III-C distinguishes quantification over a telemetry box from quantification over finite reference rows. The adapter accounts for both operands, each supporting frame and exact encoder assumptions. It uses conservative enclosures, so UNKNOWN need not prove an attainable truth flip. A concrete recorded corruption does establish one: true signed x difference −21.55 mm versus corrupted −13.96 mm; the 5-mm-per-operand enclosure crosses the −20-mm threshold. The point explanation is false while continuation succeeds.

The complementary failure is equally important: at 5 mm, all asserted invariant facts are correct but five selected continuations are inadequate, four on initially failed states. The aggregate 0/1,507 versus 123/1,953 false-literal comparison concerns three replicates of the same parents, not independent binomial trials. Noise changes decision evidence only, not feedback inside the fixed skills. No actual camera error distribution or noisy-controller safety is measured. Without a motion envelope the stale interface defers all states, which is abstention rather than completion.

## E. Are methods, units and uncertainty comparable?

The paired confirmation unit is 128 independent seed parents, carrying all five conditions and each corruption replicate. Bootstrap draws keep those groups intact. The 239 recover-only states include one preventive case, so required-recovery recall uses 238. The reference, twelve-fact diagnostics, 64 characterization parents and previous calibration are never pooled into new confirmation denominators.

B7 sees exactly the DSRE interface and ties. Completion-aware CART sees all Boolean completions rather than fewer unknown inputs. The margin control requires every input invariant and is compared with strict purity, not given a different action repertoire. Risk proposals share the same reference-error allowance, but have no new population-risk qualification. The 12-fact interface is labeled extra information. Tree explanation paths are not minimized conjunctions, so 1.49 versus 2.00 asserted literals is not an optimizer or human-usability result. The +2.03 pp CART contrast includes an additional error and descriptive, not multiplicity-adjusted, uncertainty.

Earlier clean 112-parent zero-error calibration remains valid only in its original two-condition scope. Its 6.239% bound is not imported into the new source. Same-table common-action masses are hindsight descriptors; they do not bound policies frozen on a different reference. Full-pose singleton signatures are explicitly an overfitting diagnostic.
