## Independently verified scientific outcome

**DUAL_OBLIGATION_CONFIRMED**. DSRE's non-degradation composition completes 393/640 versus 355/640 under absolute-success qualification; difference 5.94 pp [3.44, 8.91]. It selects 38/218 necessary recoveries with 0 binary harms and 4 futile attempts. The original adequate-action interpretation and gate are not relabeled.

DSRE and CART2 non-degradation decisions differ at 42/640 checkpoints; paired completion difference is 5.94 pp [3.44, 8.91]. Their recovery-assigned calibration supports are 64 and 32. Candidate sets, error rates and statistical support must explain this comparison; it is not evidence of a better controller. B7 exactly reproduces DSRE.

Our assessment: this experiment establishes the measured operational distinction only to the extent stated by its preregistered outcome label. It does **not establish a distinct new optimizer, a substantive algorithmic advantage over equivalent ordinary rules, or a general policy-improvement theorem**. The elementary error decomposition and statistical tools are established, and the new evaluator does not minimize a new harm certificate. The defensible additional contribution is source-specific evidence about which named obligation the existing observable contract can support, including its unsuccessful-attempt burden. Standalone methodological distinctiveness remains limited; a positive qualification alone does not resolve it.

No acceptance/readiness claim follows. This finite contract is complete; no further objective, optimizer, fault family, feature, controller or simulation expansion is authorized without supervisor review.

## Scientific reviewer critique

The empirical obligation distinction survives, but the method claim remains constrained. (1) B7 is exactly identical; no new rule optimizer or minimum non-degradation certificate was introduced. (2) Raw CART achieves 30 required recoveries, 0 harms and 0 futile attempts; its calibrated recovery strata fail from insufficient parent support. This is not an adverse physical comparison against CART. (3) Always-recover obtains 480/640 completions with 95 harms, while DSRE obtains 393/640 and leaves 180/218 necessary recoveries unselected. The tradeoff must be visible. (4) Zero observed selected harm and a 10% parent-conditional bound do not establish zero harm, absolute success, sensor robustness, long-horizon return or physical safety. (5) The finite reference supports scoped observable witnesses; the two counterfactual futures remain simulator/source dependent, and neither-success attempts still fail. These concerns limit standalone methodological distinctiveness even though the preregistered operational result is positive.
