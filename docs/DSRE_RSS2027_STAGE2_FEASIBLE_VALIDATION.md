## Option 1 — Contact-sensitive source feasibility on the existing insertion task

Question: can the unchanged PegInsertionSide source support an informative two-action recovery comparison at all? Current evidence is 48 valid checkpoints, q00=22/q01=25/q10=0/q11=1. Source availability passed but the locked opposing-action diversity criterion failed. It is not reasonable to promise a positive calibrated insertion result on this distribution.

A feasible narrow extension is one preregistered, independent 96-parent **source/diversity diagnostic**, with both existing macros at qualified checkpoints; no repeated batches until favorable. Estimands are the probability of an executable checkpoint, each paired outcome-class probability conditional on that checkpoint, source attrition per registered parent, and actual action/planning cost. Use exact binomial intervals at a stated family allocation; include missing endpoint identification ranges. If there are again zero continue-only parents, the two-sided 95% upper limit at n=96 is approximately .0377, not proof of zero population probability. Retain aperture UNKNOWN and all predecision/postdecision failure distinctions.

Maximum diagnostic cost: 192 future macros plus a disjoint four-parent/two-macro/two-repeat source check (16 macros), total 208; at the historical 20.723 seconds/branch this is about 1.20 worker-hours before contingency. Budget 2 worker-hours and 1 GiB, stopping at complete-parent boundaries. This cost is a planning estimate from existing measurements, not promised throughput.

Only if a separately preregistered eligibility/diversity criterion passes would a fresh D128/C1024/T256 study be scientifically meaningful. That conditional study would add 128+1024+512=1664 outcomes, about 9.58 worker-hours at the same measured rate; a 12-hour conditional cap allows limited overhead, with full counts if stopped. The diagnostic plus conditional stage is bounded at 14 worker-hours. All calibration/test parents must be fresh; the failed old 1024/256 cohort and the unused old 96 IDs are not silently reopened. If diversity fails, the conclusion is constrained repertoire/source suitability, and no new task is selected within this option.

Rebuttal-ready answer: the present paper claims two qualified manipulation mechanisms, not completed contact-rich generalization. This option directly tests the missing source and action-diversity assumptions; it cannot guarantee a successful third benchmark.

## Option 2 — Same-question evidence-budget comparison on grasping or pushing

Retain the existing macros, facts, partition procedure, harm/gain targets and confidence standard. Pick one existing domain before outcomes; do not choose whichever later produces a larger gap.

Use common fresh discovery 128 one-action parents. Freeze the partition. Compare independent final calibration arms: 1024 randomized-log parents (1024 outcomes) versus 512 uniformly selected paired parents (1024 outcomes), same whole cells, no revealed-outcome selection. Use a common independent 256-parent paired test (512 outcomes), with selected execution first. Both methods have equal calibration execution budgets and the same discovery evidence; parent budgets differ explicitly. Fixed C/R, exact B7, direct bounds, CART and existing regression receive the same admissible evidence and statistical standard. Do not infer a cross-method or best-budget joint95 guarantee.

Test intervals use independent parents and pairing; calibration claims retain `.025/(4K)` tails and the paired reserve. A zero difference is scientifically informative. The test concerns what evidence warrants, rather than whether DSRE ranks above mathematically equivalent implementations. A positive pairing advantage remains a hypothesis; absence is preserved.

## Option 3 — Measurement validity and fresh calibration under bounded observation noise

Question: does preserving factual truth under a fixed telemetry uncertainty model retain useful *scope-valid* intervention coverage? Choose existing grasping or pushing before outcomes. Keep the controllers, horizons, outcomes and six predicates fixed. Use one primary predeclared coordinate-error box radius .005 m, applied to all supporting poses/frames, and the existing interval predicate semantics. UNKNOWN remains unknown.

Collect fresh D128/C1024/T256 independent parents under one frozen measurement protocol; each D/C parent exposes one randomized outcome and T receives isolated paired scoring. Freeze the discovery partition before calibration, including the declared treatment of unrouteable/UNKNOWN records. Missingness-dependent selection must be represented in the newly calibrated source population; no old clean-cell bound is inherited by a narrower robust subset. If the current implementation cannot qualify that scope, report unsupported coverage instead of modifying the certificate theorem. Compare the same matched selectors; naive point assertions may be a descriptive factual-validity control, not a causal-safety baseline.

Estimands: false asserted literals under the specified containment model; fraction UNKNOWN/unrouteable; useful-recovery coverage; conditional harm/gain on the actual whole routed population; completions, rescues, harm and futility. Distinguish a box-containment assertion from a distributional measurement-error claim. Multiple corrupted views of a parent are dependent and never multiply the sample size. No post-hoc radius or sensor threshold selection.

Cost: 128+1024+512+16=1680 native macros. At 8.148 s grasp average this is about 3.80 worker-hours; at the conservative pushing allowance 4.67 hours. Cap at 7 worker-hours/2 GiB including source checks, prefixes and startup. Corruption and read-only scoring reuse paired test outcomes and add zero new physics. If scope support collapses, that is the recorded conclusion, not permission to add a different sensor or policy.

Rebuttal-ready answer: interval-valid facts alone do not guarantee adequate actions or source transfer. This option tests the specific observation/selection assumption with fresh independent calibration; it makes no sim-to-real or hardware commitment.
