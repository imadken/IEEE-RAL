## Fixed scientific question and source

When can actual predecision observations justify selecting a robot recovery
intervention, and what explains the failure of justification? DSRE's exact
minimum-literal certificates, typed witnesses, invariant adapter, strict-first
risk proposal and tie breaks remain unchanged. B7 and the ordinary risk wrapper
are accepted exact mathematical identities. No optimizer advantage is tested.

PickCube uses the original qualified reset and complete action-prefix replay,
including controller/RNG memory, physical parameter readbacks, contact and
sensor/state checks. Both continue and staged regrasp use their original clipped
position-feedback equations for 70 controls (3.5 s). Binary terminal task success
is the unchanged utility. A failed-start required recovery is initial failure,
continue failure and recover success. An initially successful recover-only state
is preventive, not a failed-start recovery.

The five approach-target x offsets are fixed in order: nominal 0, +.01, +.02,
+.03 and -.02 metres. They change the original scripted approach target, not
the object pose, contact material or skill library. Every parent contains all
five correlated conditions. The source distribution is the frozen simulator
reset/seed generator and these five conditions jointly. Independent seed-parent
resets and faithful source fidelity are assumptions of population inference.
No physical-world, arbitrary-checkpoint or noisy-sensor guarantee follows.

The six primary thresholds remain distances .03/.04 m, progress/displacement
.005 m, summed absolute finger positions .04 m and hand height .03 m, with the
original inequality boundaries. Pick's seventh fact is signed object-goal x
less than -.02 m. All twelve previously declared facts remain available only
to their labeled extra-information controls. No condition ID, contact/grasp
privilege or future outcome enters the decision interface.

## Frozen methods and the exact risk estimand

All 17 original methods are retained, using the same frozen model/reference:
strict DSRE, B7, strict-first DSRE risk and B7 risk, one/two-literal pure rules,
strict depth-one/two CART and pruned CART2, risk depth-one/two CART, B5 risk,
fixed continue/recover, twelve-fact full-signature strict, greedy pure rule and
policy-faithful control. The strongest primary comparisons are DSRE/B7 strict,
DSRE/B7 risk, CART2 risk, pure-two rules, B5 risk and fixed actions. No best
method is selected from new outcomes. Full signature has explicitly extra
information. The existing all-input margin comparator is descriptive only.

For every method m and action a, a parent is assigned if m proposes a in any
of its five conditions. Its error is ANY failed execution of a among those
assigned conditions. Thus n and k count distinct assigned/error parents,
not conditions or continuations. C and R are separately gated; one parent
can contribute once to each stratum. Cross-stratum independence is not assumed.

The target is conditional parent-any-assignment error <=.10, with one-sided
exact Clopper–Pearson upper bound at delta=.05/68 per claim. The earlier
68-stratum family budget is retained even though this study reports 34
new-source Pick method/action strata. No unused allocation is recycled.
Zero assignments give upper bound 1 and NOT_QUALIFIED; zero errors require
69 assigned parents. Nonzero errors use exact binomial inversion. The
production bound is independently checked against a separate SciPy CDF/root
routine. At least n=69 is a support floor, not a power or success guarantee.

Qualification requires the full 256-parent cohort, all five paired conditions,
physical/data audit success, n>0 and upper<=.10. Insufficient assigned support,
excess errors or incomplete calibration leaves the action disabled. Partial
cohort bounds, if reported, are descriptive only even when numerically small.
There is no old-data pooling. Gates veto the proposed action to STOP; they
never reroute, apply a fallback or execute an additional helpful prefix.

If full calibration completes, the fixed held-out test runs even if one or
both DSRE actions fail qualification. If calibration cannot complete within
limits, do not begin testing or claim action-specific qualification.

## Execution, resource and failure boundaries

One physics child at a time, sharing the existing nonblocking PhysX lock
`/tmp/reboot-overnight-ieeeral.lock`; never truncate/delete it or interfere with
other jobs. Each child has a 330-s internal deadline and 360-s external timeout.
Chunks admit at most eight parents and stop admitting when a 360-s reserve
would exceed 840 s. Complete per-parent progress and decisions are atomic.
All failed, partial and interrupted attempts are retained; never rerun a started
parent or replace its seed. A physical/provenance mismatch halts fresh physics.
Only a documented preflight serialization correction, with an immutable failed
attempt and new source seal, may precede a fresh qualification attempt; it
cannot alter scientific semantics or occur after calibration starts.

Limits: 14 total campaign worker-wall hours, 12 new physics wall hours,
20 measured CPU core-hours, 6,000,000,000 new artifact bytes; no model training,
paid API or unrelated download. Conservatively count total elapsed wall from
the first preflight at 2026-11-16T10:21:10Z. Include qualification reruns in
physics usage. Reserve one wall-hour and two CPU core-hours for independent
analysis, audit and closure. Chunks measure all physics-child CPU including
imports; separate metered offline commands record child CPU and wall time.
An additional conservative 600-CPU-second cap debit covers unmetered initial
document search, setup, seal checks and preflight; it is not called measured CPU.
GPU model training is forbidden. No new parent starts without timeout/artifact
reserve. Resource checks are outcome-independent and persist across resumes.

At a limit, retain the current bounded attempt and exact completed denominators.
If interrupted, leave a sealed incomplete-cohort record and explicit commands
for never-started parents only. An incomplete parent stays unavailable; it does
not become a full sample by dropping a condition or replacing a seed. A complete
calibration is mandatory to launch any test future. Do not promise execution
after the host/session terminates.
