# DSRE v1.2 scientific readiness decision

**Decision: BOUNDED_STAGE1_SCIENCE.** All authorized experimental stages and
independently executable analyses are complete. The scientific core now separates
measurement-stable assertions, finite-reference sufficiency and physical-action
feasibility with prospective evidence. It does not establish a new optimizer,
unique superiority over ordinary rules, or noisy/OOD population qualification.
This is an internal scientific assessment, not external peer review or a
prediction of RSS acceptance. Neither manuscript is revised or submitted.

The experiment is not an uninformative one-action population: all four paired

strong, general robotics method.

## Adversarial scientific assessment

1. **Ordinary-rule equivalence limits algorithmic novelty.** Strict DSRE/B7 and
   their strict-first risk wrappers are identities for every partial query, not
   merely nonsignificant empirical ties. Witness diagnostics do not turn the
   underlying search into a new optimizer. The primary-source matrix includes
   DA-GRD, RESCUE and counterfactual recovery-tree work that constrain broad
   novelty claims.

2. **The new CART difference has an error tradeoff.** On 640 confirmation states,
   DSRE risk gives 401 adequate/two inadequate/237 STOP versus CART2 risk
   388/one/251. The paired adequate-credit effect is +2.03125 percentage points,
   95% CI [0.9375, 3.28125], but with one additional inadequate intervention.
   B7 risk reproduces the whole DSRE result. Depth-one/two frozen trees do not
   establish superiority over the general class of decision trees.

3. **Most risk-versus-strict improvement is maintenance.** The additional 50 good
   decisions are all maintenance of existing success; necessary recovery remains
   48 for both tiers. Of the risk policy's 50 proposed recoveries, 48 are
   necessary, one is both-success maintenance and one fails where both skills
   fail. The matched R-minus-C effect in this selected subset is 48/50 = 96%,
   grouped CI [89.5806%, 100%]. This is a conditional simulator effect, not a
   population guarantee or evidence for a new controller.

4. **Factual robustness is not action safety.** At 5 mm, invariant risk evidence
   supplies 368 adequate/five inadequate/267 STOP decisions and only 15/238
   necessary recoveries (6.3025%, CI [2.1004%, 11.4179%]). All five inadequate
   decisions are continuations on recover-only states, four initially unsuccessful
   and one preventive. All asserted invariant facts are valid. A correct
   explanation interface can still use an inadequate finite reference under shift.

5. **The subset mechanism is useful but not unique.** At 5 mm, strict DSRE retains
   280 adequate decisions and 15 necessary recoveries versus the all-input margin
   rule's 148 and one, while adding one inadequate decision. The adequate-credit
   difference is +20.625 pp, CI [18.28125, 23.125]. Robust B7 exactly matches it.
   This tests using a sufficient stable subset; it does not discover a new robust
   inference algorithm. Conservative bounds and Boolean completion supersets can
   themselves cause avoidable abstention.

6. **Fixed-action competitors expose the objective tradeoff.** Fixed recovery
   attains 523 raw successes, exceeding DSRE risk's 401, but has 117 inadequate
   interventions, including 69 harms on continue-only states. Fixed continuation
   succeeds 353 times. STOP receives zero selective credit, with no invented
   terminal-hold outcome. No scalar utility or new score suppresses these facts.

7. **Physical variation is narrow and its semantics matter.** The five conditions
   vary the existing scripted approach target, not object pose, materials or
   skills. They form a useful controlled source shift within one PickCube task;
   the old Push evidence is reused offline. The study does not validate new
   objects, hardware, learned perception or a general recovery repertoire.

8. **Sensor and risk scopes remain open.** Error boxes are declared absolute
   coordinate bounds on idealized telemetry; finger encoders are assumed exact.
   Noise changes decision evidence, not feedback inside the fixed controllers.
   A timestamped past pose has no current-state guarantee without a motion
   envelope. The original 112-parent/zero-error 6.239% bound stays valid only in
   its original clean two-condition scope. No new gate is fitted: even 64
   hypothetical error-free characterization parents yield a 10.6615% upper bound
   at the reserved .05/68 standard, above the 10% target. Confirmation labels are
   never used for calibration or condition selection.

9. **Ceilings are diagnostic, not deployed scores.** The 640 complete-pose
   signatures are singletons. Exact ternary-pattern partition masses and the
   separate wildcard-compatible statistic have different semantics. Neither is
   a generalization bound for a policy frozen on another reference. The per-arm
   +2/−2 cm observation-mass difference (65 versus 14 with equal 115 feasible
   states) is a descriptive representational/source asymmetry; hidden arm labels
   are never supplied to the policies.

The remaining evidence needed for stronger claims includes independently
qualified sensor/source-specific risk support and broader physical/perception
validation under the same question. Those requirements do not authorize further
experiments in this completed sprint. The next manuscript decision belongs to
the supervisor; this session produces research evidence and figures only.
