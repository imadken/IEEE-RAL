## 1. Explicit decision

**METHOD_EQUIVALENT_AND_NOT_DISTINCTLY_MOTIVATED**

The finite-model theory, exact implementation, independent oracles and recorded
observation-capability witness pass. The stronger originality gate does not.
ERR adds a minimum identifying-type plan to the old certificate-only interface,
but a fully instrumented B7 implementation is identical. More importantly,
equivalence-class/decision-region determination and minimal model-testing
experiments are direct antecedents of the generic mechanics. This conclusion
does not follow merely from a B7 tie, which the supervisor expected. It follows
from the prior-art challenge plus the absence of a demonstrated new robot
identification regime or acquisition benefit in the fixed evidence.

This does not refute the usefulness of the recovery-evidence application
question. It means that the current evidence does not warrant presenting the
generic finite-cover machinery as a newly established robotics methodology.
An application/hypothesis paper remains a supervisor judgment. No new research
direction or experiment campaign is initiated to rescue a preferred verdict.
