## Evidence-backed objection matrix

### O1. “DSRE is just ordinary rules with a new name.”

**Correct:** B7 is structurally identical under matched vocabulary, compatibility, support and ties; matched risk wrappers also reproduce actions. **Existing answer:** SectionIII-A/Proposition1 defines the evidence object and its conditional soundness; III-B adds typed proof objects, IV separates obligation eligibility, and V tests their different physical consequences. LedgerF04–F14, not a score comparison, carries the distinction.

**Supported response:** “We claim an intervention-grounded evidence contract and its mechanism-separating validation, not a new pure-rule optimizer. Equivalent B7 receives the same result. The contract states what a finite explanation and a population qualification separately warrant.” **Unresolved:** Whether this formulation is a sufficient methodological advance over the closest combination remains a genuine review risk. **Possible Charter obligation:** A bounded formal comparison on the fixed two-action, at-most12-literal vocabulary: map outputs of ordinary rules, finite feasibility intersection and DSRE, identify exactly which typed outputs add information, and supply counterexamples where they do not. No controller or optimizer change.

### O2. “The new CART comparison rewards more calibration assignments.”

**Correct:** DSRE has64 recovery-assigned calibration parents; CART has32. CART observes no absolute or harm errors but its13.896% upper bound fails10%. **Existing answer:** TableI and SectionV-A expose this; TableII reports raw CART385 completions and30 required rescues with no harm or futility. B7 ties DSRE exactly. LedgerN04,N10.

**Supported response:** “Calibrated refusal is support-limited, not evidence of worse physical proposals. We compare identical observations, calibration opportunity and risk standards, and retain raw performance.” **Unresolved:** Shallow fixed CART is not every outcome-aware tree; sample assignment affects qualification. **Possible Charter obligation:** A separately preregistered fixed-support analysis of the existing frozen policies, with one independent cohort and no outcome-driven enrichment; determine support/power before labels. Do not transfer current intervals or refit on these test labels.

### O3. “Non-degradation is being sold as safety.”

**Correct:** Only binary terminal success relative to continue is measured. No collision, damage or energy endpoint is qualified. **Existing answer:** Eq.3, SectionIV and the page5 limitations; TableII retains four futile DSRE retries. D's0/64 parent harm bound is7.208%, not zero;0/42 test harm is descriptive.

**Supported response:** “The label is task-outcome non-degradation only. Neither-success still fails the task; original task-adequacy semantics are unchanged.” **Unresolved:** Physical hazard and cost are unmeasured. **Possible Charter obligation:** First define one independently measurable physical-hazard endpoint and its source-specific risk design before collecting a single outcome. At most one qualified task/source; do not reinterpret current binary success as hazard evidence.

### O4. “The two gates changed alpha or used the test labels.”

No new population claim from existing data.

## Three skeptical internal reviewer simulations

These are separate analytical perspectives produced internally, not three external or human reviews. Each addresses the actual five pages and does not assign a probability of acceptance.

### A. Formal RL/MDP and sufficient explanations

**Reject concern:** The intersection criterion, minimum conjunction and elementary error decomposition are established. B7 is identical; the non-degradation gate has no newly optimized explanation. A reviewer can reasonably regard the methodological increment as too small.

**Evidence-grounded answer:** SectionsIII–IV give a precise reference-coverage assumption, literal necessity proof, three diagnostic types and distinct gate quantifiers. Fig.1 and V-B show why a physical neither-success witness differs from aliasing. These establish a coherent, scoped evidence object and an empirical characterization, not a stronger optimization theorem.

**Disposition:** G2 is defensible; G1 remains at risk. An answer asserting a new minimizer or general safe-policy theorem would be unsupported. The bounded proof/output comparison in O1/O9/O10 is a possible later Charter item, not completed evidence.

### B. Robotics manipulation and intervention semantics

**Reject concern:** A single parameter family, idealized observations and historical Push examples fall short of broadly useful robotic recovery; fixed recovery completes more tasks and the selective method misses180 required cases.

**Evidence-grounded answer:** IV-B fully specifies skills, horizon, thresholds and paired replay. TableII preserves the fixed baseline's480 successes and95 harms, while38 selected rescues all start unsuccessful; the gain is not maintenance or an extra useful prefix. Page5 bounds scope and separates factual stability from controller performance.

**Disposition:** The controlled mechanism is scientifically meaningful; hardware/general recovery superiority is unestablished. A narrow Stage1 argument can be considered on that basis, while breadth remains a legitimate reason to reject stronger claims. O5/O7/O8 define falsifiable, limited validations if requested later.

### C. Statistical calibration and experimental science

**Reject concern:** Conditional support differences, clustered conditions and the weaker harm endpoint could manufacture a favorable comparison. Zero observed harm can be misleading.

**Evidence-grounded answer:** Eq.4 defines the independent parent unit and any-condition error; TableI uses the same64 DSRE assignments for both obligations and the same alpha. CART's32-parent support and raw30 rescues are visible. Protocol and timing seals exclude test adaptation. The primary5.94pp interval resamples parents, while the0/42 test harm remains descriptive and the7.21% bound remains source conditional.

**Disposition:** G3 is defensible within the registered source; no general safe-policy-return, hardware or noise guarantee follows. Additional tests cannot retroactively enrich these cohorts. All confidence and policy-scope caveats belong in the main five pages, as they now are.
